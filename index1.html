GALAXY

A Galaxy is a vast system of stars, planets, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity. Our universe contains billions of galaxies, each with its own structure, history, and fascinating features. Galaxies can take different forms,including spiral,elliptical, and irregular shapes, with spiral galaxies such as the "Milky Way" displaying beautiful arms filled with stars and cosmic clouds.

At the center of many galaxies lies a supermassive black hole, surrounded by enormous amounts of matter and energy. Studying galaxies allows scientists to understand how stars are born, how planetary systems develop, and how the universe has evolved over billions of years. The Milky Way itself contains hundreds of billions of stars, including our Sun, and is part of a larger group of galaxies known as the Local Group. With powerful telescopes and space observatories, astronomers continue to discover distant galaxies that existed billions of years ago.

Exploring galaxies therefore means looking not only into distant space, but also into the history and origins of our universe.
